About

Architecture that speaks both business and system

Product catalog is where commercial strategy meets system reality. I sit in that gap: translating what marketing wants to sell into offers, products, services, pricing and rules that billing, ordering and charging can actually execute — then defending that model through design reviews, migrations and production.

My background is deliberately hybrid. I came up writing Java conversion and reconciliation tooling for billing migrations, moved through business analysis and design, owned the CatalogONE product backlog for five years, and now architect it for a North American tier-1. That lets me hold a customer conversation and a code-level design discussion in the same meeting.
